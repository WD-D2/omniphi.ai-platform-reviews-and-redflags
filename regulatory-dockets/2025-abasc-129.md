# 🏛️ Regulatory Enforcement Docket: Re Floreani, 2025 ABASC 129

### 📋 Case Information
* **Jurisdiction:** Alberta Securities Commission (ASC) [1]
* **Ruling Date:** September 26, 2025 [1]
* **Citation Number:** 2025 ABASC 129 [1]
* **Primary Subjects:** James Domenic Floreani, Jayconomics Inc. [1]
* **Corporate Nexus:** Levitee Labs Inc. (Formerly CSE: LVT) [1]

---

### 🔍 Core Findings of the Commission
The Alberta Securities Commission panel formally adjudicated that a sophisticated, undisclosed promotional ring was deployed to artificially inflate retail demand for public micro-cap equities. 

1. **Undisclosed Paid Promotions:** The ASC proved that leadership networks within Levitee Labs entered into covert financial agreements with online financial influencers (specifically targeting retail channels across YouTube, X/Twitter, and Patreon).
2. **Infraction Categories:** The subjects were found in violation of **Section 103.1(2) of the Securities Act**, which strictly prohibits engaging in investor relations and promotional activities without clear, conspicuous, and concurrent disclosure of the paid commercial relationship.
3. **Execution Mode:** Paid "Buy Alerts" and urgent market entries were pushed to retail investors while insiders and connected operational actors maintained exit liquidity positioning.

---

### 🔨 Sanctions & Penalties Imposed
The Commission handed down severe market integrity penalties to deter future operational mimicry:
* **Market Prohibition:** A mandatory **two-year capital market ban** restricting the subjects from trading or advising in securities.
* **Administrative Fines:** A flat financial penalty of **$30,000**.
* **Cost Recovery:** An order to pay **$10,185.10** covering the direct costs of the forensic investigation.

---

### ⛓️ Direct Lineage to Algoriphi, Inc. (OmniPhi)
Open-source corporate tracking registers confirm that **Pouya Farmand** (Co-Founder & President of OmniPhi / Algoriphi, Inc.) served directly as the **Co-Founder, CEO, and Director of Levitee Labs Inc.** during the exact operational timeline investigated by the ASC. 

When Levitee Labs imploded and was officially **delisted by the Canadian Securities Exchange in January 2026**, the core management network abandoned the shell, pivoted to the Silicon Valley artificial intelligence boom, and deployed an identical promotional framework under the guise of an unregulated software provider layer.

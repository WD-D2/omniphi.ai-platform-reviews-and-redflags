# 📉 Corporate Transparency Ledger: Beeks Financial Cloud Partnership Metrics

### 🔍 Executive Overview
On October 19, 2026, Beeks Financial Cloud Group plc (AIM: BKS) publicly announced a strategic partnership and minority equity investment in Algoriphi, Inc. (OmniPhi) under the service title **"Beeks Sovereign AI"**. This documentation establishes a factual cross-reference between the public PR narrative and the audited full-year financial statements released concurrently by Beeks Group.

### 💵 Part 1: Capital Allocation & Audited Cash Balances
While top-line marketing outlines an 11% revenue expansion to £40.0M, Beeks' official FY26 financial ledger (released October 19, 2026) highlights a significant structural drawdown in liquid capital reserves:

* **Net Cash Compression:** Beeks' audited net cash reserves dropped by **91.4%**, declining from **£7.0 million down to £0.6 million** year-over-year.
* **Debt Expansion Profile:** The compression reflects significant upfront hardware deployment costs. To bridge expenditures, Beeks expanded its liabilities by drawing a **£1.5 million bank loan** and increasing its asset finance obligations to **£3.2 million** (up from £0.4 million the previous year).
* **Market Valuation Multiple:** Despite a choppy trading quarter where shares corrected down to **~183p** from a 52-week peak of **270p**, Beeks commands an enterprise growth premium trading at a trailing **P/E multiple of ~37x to 40x**. Independent financial tracking notes that management requires strong forward-looking software catalogs to defend this valuation multiple amid the reduction in cash liquidity.

### ⏱️ Part 2: Agreement Structure & Deployment Horizons
Factual analysis of the corporate disclosures surrounding the joint enterprise reveals unique structural deferrals:

1. **Undisclosed Financial Considerations:** Across all official transaction logs, the precise capital consideration amount, common share volumes, and equity ownership percentages acquired by Beeks remain **withheld under corporate confidentiality terms**. 
2. **Back-Weighted Deployment Timeline:** According to official product roadmaps, the "Beeks Sovereign AI" enterprise configuration is scheduled for availability in the **third fiscal quarter of 2027 (Q3 Calendar Year 2027)**. This maps a 12-month forward runway before the system generates active commercial revenue.
3. **Enterprise Contract Assets & Billing:** Beeks' disclosures note a balance of **£17.7 million in contracted billings** to be collected over long-term customer cycles. By pegging the OmniPhi partnership to an on-premise "Sovereign AI" installation running locally on client servers, the software structure minimizes centralized cloud operation liabilities for both entities.
